### 💡 Design Tips for GitHub Profiles

* **Consistency is Key:** If you use the table for languages, I highly recommend using **Option 1**. Having two different styles of tables can make the profile feel cluttered.
* **The "Pin" Strategy:** Instead of putting every detail in your README, consider **Pinning** 6 repositories that represent these skills. For example, pin a repo where you did a CTF (Binary Exploitation) right next to your skills table.
* **Accessibility:** Screen readers sometimes struggle with the special characters (▰▰▱). Always make sure the **text** (e.g., "Foundational") is there so everyone can understand your level.
